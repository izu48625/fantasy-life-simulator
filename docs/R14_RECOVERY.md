# R1.4 — Rehabilitation & Second Careers

- Treatment imposes genuine medical leave. Return examinations are deferred and can fail.
- After failure, at least two years of recovery are required before retry. Characters can instead become training instructors.
- Rear-duty veterans and military retirees may begin mentorship careers with tracked years and trainee counts.
- Salary/risk varies between rehabilitation, front line, rear support and instructor roles.
- Existing save format v1 remains valid; missing R1.4 fields are treated as absent.
